ACCESS & COMMERCIAL MODEL

Pricing and early access.
Clarity before commitments.

Searqon is in early alpha. Public subscription tiers and production service commitments have not been published because customer validation, provider permissions and operating costs are still being established.

AVAILABLE NOW

Open-source toolkit

The local CLI and assessment engine are available under Apache-2.0. You can inspect the code, run supported assessments and generate local reports without buying a hosted plan.

View source on GitHub →

EARLY ALPHA

Hosted workspace foundation

Authentication, organizations, projects and persisted assessments exist in the codebase. A generally available hosted service, service-level commitments and paid subscriptions are not yet offered.

Review current security boundaries →

WHY NO PUBLIC PRICE LIST?

Price follows validated
value and delivery cost.

Reliable pricing depends on real usage, provider terms, collection costs, storage, support requirements and customer outcomes. Publishing arbitrary tiers before those inputs are validated would be misleading.

No payment is required to use the open-source toolkit.

Do not interpret the early-access registration flow as an offer of a paid subscription or a production service commitment.
