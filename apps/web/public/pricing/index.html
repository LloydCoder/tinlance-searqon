Usage notifications

Usage notifications are defined at 80% and 100% of included allowances, with reserved, settled and overage usage distinguished.

No unused usage rollover: unused monthly allowances do not roll over. Non-metered limits such as projects, seats, prompts and engines block at the cap and require an upgrade or contract; they do not silently become paid overages.

HOW USAGE IS COUNTED

Clear units. Auditable events.

AI answer check

One successful normalized answer observation for one prompt × one engine × one locale × one run. A three-engine check consumes three units. Failed acquisition attempts and idempotent retries are not billable.

Crawl page

One distinct authorized page successfully fetched and processed within the declared scope. Robots-denied, blocked, rejected and failed fetches are not billable. Authorization and SSRF controls still apply.

Tracked keyword-month

One keyword × locale × device target for one subscription month. A new locale or device target is a separate unit.

API request

One successful billable customer API operation. Health checks, internal calls, rejected authentication, validation failures and idempotent duplicate deliveries are excluded.

Usage is aligned to the subscription billing period. Trial answer-check usage is a lifetime counter for that trial and does not reset monthly. Currency calculations use integer micro-USD to avoid floating-point billing errors.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you choose.

Is the pricing live and can I subscribe today?

No. These are the proposed hosted packages and rates already defined in Searqon's pricing policy. Paid checkout and subscription activation are not currently available. They will remain unavailable until payment, usage enforcement, product capability and commercial-readiness gates are complete.

Is the open-source toolkit paid?

No. The local/open-source toolkit remains available under Apache-2.0. The hosted package proposal does not paywall the repository or its supported local assessment workflow.

What happens when a trial ends?

The workspace moves to Free. There is no automatic conversion or charge. Paid-only scheduled work is paused, and existing data follows the applicable retention and deletion policy.

Do I lose data when I downgrade?

No. Downgrade does not silently delete projects, evidence or audit records. Items above the new plan limits may become read-only until the workspace is brought within its allowance.

Can Enterprise customers receive custom limits?

Eligible limits and features can be adjusted through a signed, active Enterprise contract with an approved internal approver and an auditable validity period. Platform hard safety ceilings remain in force. Residency, dedicated environments and service commitments also require operational approval.

Are the overage rates final?

They are the proposed retail rates in the current pricing policy. They are not provider-cost estimates and will not be activated until representative usage, cost-to-serve, provider terms and gross margins have been validated.
