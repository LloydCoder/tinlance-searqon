ABOUT SEARQON

Understand visibility.
Engineer discoverability. Enable agents.

Searqon is an evidence-first toolkit for understanding how digital properties are discovered, represented and used across search engines, answer engines, generative AI systems and authorized agents.

OUR POINT OF VIEW

Discoverability is
an engineering problem.

01

Evidence first

Claims should trace to a source, timestamp, observation class and method.

02

Measured, not guessed

Coverage, confidence and uncertainty belong beside findings. No unexplained magic scores.

03

Safe by design

Finding an endpoint does not authorize access. People retain authority over credentials, tools and changes.

BUILT BY TINLANCE

Open foundations.
Careful product evolution.

Searqon is developed by Tinlance. The public toolkit is licensed under Apache-2.0 and is designed to remain useful locally without paid APIs or cloud credentials. The hosted product direction remains subject to customer validation, security review and explicit operational readiness gates.
